About Our School

PM SHRI School Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Kokrajhar is a co-educational residential school under the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti, Ministry of Education, Government of India. Established in 1995, the school is located at Ranchaidham, Basugaon, Chirang District, Assam.

As a proud PM SHRI School, we are committed to becoming an exemplar institution that fully implements the vision of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 — focusing on holistic, experiential, and skill-based education.

About This Website

jnvkokrajharprojects.in is the official digital platform to showcase the innovative projects created by the students of PM SHRI JNV Kokrajhar.

From IoT-based water management systems and sustainable agriculture models to waste management solutions and health & hygiene initiatives, every project displayed here reflects creativity, scientific temper, and a strong problem-solving mindset.

This platform celebrates student innovation, highlights achievements at Cluster, Regional and National levels, and inspires more young minds to innovate.

PM SHRI Vision & Skill Education

Under the PM SHRI (PM Schools for Rising India) scheme, our school focuses on developing well-rounded individuals equipped with 21st-century skills.

Key focus areas include:

  • Experiential and hands-on learning
  • Integration of Artificial Intelligence, Robotics & Coding
  • Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) for innovation and design thinking
  • Skill education and vocational exposure
  • Environmental sustainability and green practices
  • Holistic development through sports, arts and values

The Atal Tinkering Lab in our school provides students access to 3D printers, electronics, sensors, robotics kits and IoT tools — enabling them to turn ideas into real prototypes and solutions for local and global challenges.

Our Vision

To nurture curious, creative and confident young innovators who can use science, technology and human values to solve real-world problems and contribute meaningfully to the vision of a developed and self-reliant India (Viksit & Atmanirbhar Bharat).
